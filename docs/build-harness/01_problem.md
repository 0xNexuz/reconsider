# Problem

Status: PLANNED; user demand UNVERIFIED.

Primary user: an apparel shopper disappointed by style who wants to evaluate a replacement. Buyer/operator: a small apparel merchant. Reviewer: a judge or pilot merchant.

Today, return portals offer catalog alternatives and price settlement; the research brief cites ReturnGO and Loop. Our hypothesis is that some shoppers need a personal visual comparison before selecting an alternative. No quantified loss, saving, or adoption metric is established.

Success means the shopper can understand eligible choices and make a voluntary, informed decision. Non-goals: exact size prediction, replacing physical fit checks, medical advice, autonomous purchases, and universal store scraping.

Validation: interview three merchants and five recent returners. Ask what caused their last return, what information was missing, and whether a preview would have changed their decision. Record negative evidence too. Stop or revise if style uncertainty is not a meaningful cause for the selected audience.
